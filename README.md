## 🧭 About Me

I work where **physics, math, and computer science** meet — quantum error correction and hybrid optimization on one side, production machine learning and autonomous agent systems on the other.

Most of what I do comes down to the same thing: making a system's real behaviour visible. That means building the control that tells you *which* component failed, catching the data leakage or unconstrained edge case in your own result before someone else does, and reporting the number you got rather than the number you wanted.

I like to:
- Design and benchmark **quantum error correction decoders and optimization pipelines** at the circuit level — `[[n, k, d]]` qLDPC syndrome decoding (`Stim`, BP+OSD), 3-body HUBO/QUBO formulations, and variational/counterdiabatic solvers
- Build **spec-driven AI & ML systems**: explicit contracts (`BRIEF.md` → `CONTRACT.md`), deterministic test gates, distributed multi-GPU training, and live deployments
- Turn ideas from **papers, hackathons, and research cohorts** into working, measured prototypes that run within real hardware constraints
- Write things up so a reader can **check the claims** from a clean clone instead of taking them on trust

## 🚀 What I'm Building

### 🛡️ qecscreen — [repo](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/qecscreen)

Circuit-level screening harness for quantum LDPC code search. Replaces the analytical $kd^2/n$ proxy with learned ranking over `Stim` circuit-level syndrome simulations so candidate codes are filtered by actual logical error behavior before burning full Monte Carlo budgets. Milestone 0 is closed and published (`Apache-2.0`, `CITATION.cff`, `NARRATIVE.md`); Milestone 1 ranking benchmarks are next.

### ⚡ Pramana AI (प्रमाण) — [live](https://pramana-ai-eta.vercel.app) · [pitch deck](https://pramana-ai-eta.vercel.app/deck/index.html) · [repo](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/pramana-ai)

Autonomous multimodal checkout QA, conversion-leakage, and self-healing engineering agent built with Team Vimarsha for **Startup Buildathon India 2026**. Currently executing a 9-step clean-core rebuild with a standalone `ScheduleValidator` strictly separated from `SolverStatus`.

## 🔬 Selected Work & Benchmarks

### 🧮 `[[18,4,4]]` qLDPC Syndrome Decoder — Kaggle QEC Decoder Hackathon 2026
Custom from-scratch `float32` normalized Min-Sum Belief Propagation (5 iterations, $\alpha = 0.8$) + GF(2) Ordered Statistics Decoding (`OSD-0`) built in pure `NumPy`, `SciPy`, `Numba`, and `Stim` without external QEC libraries (`ldpc` or `pymatching`), using 10,000-shot chunked decoding to stay within an 8 GB RAM ceiling under SI1000 ($p = 0.002$) circuit-level depolarizing noise. Achieved **0.04446 Public / 0.04483 Private per-observable MAE** (~6.5× lower error than the ~0.29 baseline; local validation shot LER **0.079915** across 200,000 sampled shots).

`Repo:` [qec-decoder-hackathon-2026](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/qec-decoder-hackathon-2026)

### ⚛️ Quantum Document Pipeline — QIntern 2026, QWorld
FRQI/NEQR encoding, a quanvolutional feature layer, and a Grover comparator oracle, benchmarked against Tesseract with 30-split significance testing. An earlier run of mine reported a +1.4 point gain — the angle search had touched rows that later appeared in the test set, and re-running with a sealed partition brought it to +0.1. Both numbers are in the report.

Reproduces from a clean clone in under a minute. Scoped for three people; all 23 commits are mine.

`Repo:` [qi26_25](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/qi26_25) · [Live demo](https://pushkar0997.github.io/qi26_25/) · [Report](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/qi26_25/blob/main/docs/final_report.md)

### 🧬 RNA Folding with Quantum Optimization — WISER × Moderna challenge
CVaR-VQE reproduces ViennaRNA's exact minimum-free-energy structure at a 0.000 kcal/mol gap, holding across 35 runs under shot and depolarizing noise. Where it missed, exhaustive brute force over the same QUBO missed identically — proving the limitation was in the formulation, not the optimizer.

`Repo:` [Moderna-Quantum-RNA](https://github.com/Harold-Ohandja/Moderna-Quantum-RNA-) · [Interactive explainer](https://harold-ohandja.github.io/Moderna-Quantum-RNA-/)

### ⚡ Distributed Training on GCP Vertex AI & Veritas AI v2
Multi-GPU Longformer fine-tuning with a self-relaunching `accelerate` topology, rank-gated tokenization behind a barrier file, explicit NCCL configuration, and a four-destination checkpoint strategy. Plus a cheap smoke test that validates IAM and GCS access before any GPU spins up — written after a silent deadlock ran unattended for twenty hours and produced nothing. Powers **Veritas AI v2**, which fixes v1's train/inference global attention mask mismatch across ISOT, LIAR, and FEVER.

`Repos:` [vertex-pytorch-trainers](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/vertex-pytorch-trainers) · [veritas_ai_v2](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/veritas_ai_v2) · [HF Model](https://huggingface.co/PushkarKumar/veritas_ai_v2)

### 🌿 Plant Disease Detection (DenseNet-169)
Two-stage pipeline: contour/HSV localisation, then DenseNet-169 across 15 PlantVillage classes. 97.8% accuracy, 97.7% macro F1 on 4,134 images — though validation drove checkpoint selection, so that number is optimistically biased and I say so. The confidence floor was chosen by measurement: my first instinct of 0.40 would have suppressed zero errors, so it ships at 0.80.

`Repo:` [plant-disease-densenet169](https://github.com/Pushkar0997/plant-disease-densenet169) · [Demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/PushkarKumar/plant-disease-densenet169) · [Model](https://huggingface.co/PushkarKumar/plant-disease-densenet169)
